# Grundfos CR, CRI, CRN 60 Hz data booklet

The booklet groups performance curves and technical data by pump model. Match the exact model and frequency before comparing measured flow and head. Inlet pressure, operating pressure, liquid temperature, and shaft-seal limits constrain the comparison.

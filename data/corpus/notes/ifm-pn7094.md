# ifm PN7094 pressure sensor datasheet

The PN7094 measures relative pressure from −1 to 10 bar and supports switching outputs and IO-Link. Check the configured output mode before converting readings into engineering units. Distinguish a low process pressure from a scaling, output configuration, or wiring problem. Verify the process connection and suitability for the measured fluid before comparing sensors.

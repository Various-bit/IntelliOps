# ifm VNB001 vibration sensor datasheet

The VNB001 measures vibration velocity in mm/s over a specified frequency range of 2 to 1,000 Hz. Its maximum specified velocity depends on frequency. Confirm the measurement unit, configuration, mounting position, and orientation before interpreting a change in vibration. A vibration excursion warrants correlation with pressure, flow, and pump operating state.

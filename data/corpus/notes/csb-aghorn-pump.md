# US CSB Aghorn Operating waterflood station investigation

A pump failure released produced water containing hydrogen sulfide. The investigation could not establish whether failure preceded the worker's arrival or occurred when the pump energized during isolation. Keep that uncertainty in the incident timeline and distinguish equipment failure from the contributing isolation and detection failures.

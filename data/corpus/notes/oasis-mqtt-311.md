# OASIS MQTT 3.1.1 standard

Keep Alive, Clean Session, retained publications, and QoS govern common reconnect and delivery behavior. Verify session settings and subscription QoS when readings disappear after reconnecting. Use the telemetry timestamp to assess the freshness of retained readings.

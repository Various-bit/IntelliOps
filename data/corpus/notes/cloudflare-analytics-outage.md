# Cloudflare postmortem on the November 2023 control plane and analytics outage

A data-center power failure affected the control plane and analytics. Hidden dependencies blocked some failover paths, and some logs were lost even while network traffic continued to flow. Compare service health with observability health when interpreting missing metrics or logs.

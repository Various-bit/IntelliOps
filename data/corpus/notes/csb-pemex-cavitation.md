# US CSB Incident Reports Volume One, case 21, Pemex Deer Park refinery fire

The investigation links a low liquid level upstream during startup to low suction pressure and cavitation. The resulting vibration damaged the bearings. Several days later the mechanical seal failed and released hydrocarbons that ignited. Compare operating history with the later leak rather than treating the leak as an isolated event.
